- Integrity: `xnu-12377.121.6.sha256` lists the SHA-256 of every imported
  file, relative to `xnu-12377.121.6/`.
- License: `APPLE_LICENSE` (the Apple Public Source License, version 2.0) is

  (`APPLE_OSREFERENCE_LICENSE_HEADER` or `APPLE_LICENSE_HEADER`) except

  carries a Carnegie Mellon University and Apple notice; and
  `config/MASTER.x86_64` and `config/MASTER.arm64.MacOSX` carry no notice.
  The tree's `APPLE_LICENSE` applies to all of them. Many Mach and BSD
  files (`bsd/kern/kern_exec.c`, `bsd/kern/subr_xxx.c`, `bsd/sys/errno.h`,
  the `osfmk/ipc/` sources, and most `osfmk/mach/` headers and `.defs`
  files) also retain Carnegie Mellon University or University of
  California notices. The files are reference material for an independent
  implementation; no RAX source is derived from their text.

Paths under `xnu-12377.121.6/` mirror the XNU tree. The files are reference
inputs; do not reformat or edit them. To move to a newer release, import a
complete new `xnu-<version>/` tree with its own provenance record.

The host this work was developed on runs a newer, unpublished kernel
(`xnu-13432`, macOS 27.2). Where its behavior visibly differs from this
release, the difference is recorded next to the implementation rather than
inferred from these files.
