## Hexagon opcode data

`src/isa/hexagon/generated/opcodes.rs` is generated by
`tools/hexagon/gen_iset.py` from QEMU v9.0.0 Hexagon encoding definitions and
attributes. The input files in `tools/hexagon/qemu/` carry Qualcomm copyright
notices and GPL-2.0-or-later license terms.
